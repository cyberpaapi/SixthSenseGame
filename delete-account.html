Request deletion of your Sixth Sense account or data

Sixth Sense : Word Puzzle is published by AlphaCodeAI. The app's existing support link is labelled Sensei.

You can request deletion of your reserved multiplayer username/profile and its associated service-held data. You can also request deletion of specific multiplayer data while keeping your username.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, write to aryanchandwani@gmail.com with the subject Sixth Sense account or data deletion. You do not need to reinstall the game.

What to include

  1. Your Sixth Sense username and whether you want the entire profile deleted or only specific multiplayer data.
  2. If relevant, the room code and approximate date of the match.

Do not send passwords, payment details or your full recovery code in the initial email. We may need to verify that you control the profile before deleting it, to protect other players from unauthorized requests. We will reply with the verification steps and confirm completion.

How to request deletion from the app

Open Settings → Contact Sensei support and send the same request. The in-app privacy policy also explains how to contact us about deletion.

What is deleted and what may remain

  • After an account-deletion request is verified, we delete the reserved username/profile record, recovery-code hash and associated service-held multiplayer identity, progress and activity data. The username becomes available again. Deletion does not erase unrelated players' accounts.
  • Solo coins, progress, settings and avatars are stored on your device. To remove these too, clear Sixth Sense's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. A support request cannot remotely erase local storage.
  • Multiplayer rooms normally expire after 24 hours, but expiration is not itself a guarantee of database deletion. Short-lived rate-limit records are removed on subsequent requests after one day. Account deletion requests are handled separately from that routine expiry.
  • Purchase records may be retained where necessary to support a transaction or meet legal obligations. Deleting a game profile does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. Google and advertising providers handle their own records under their privacy policies.
