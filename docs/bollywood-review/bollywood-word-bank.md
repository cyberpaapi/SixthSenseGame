# Sixth Sense — Guess the movie or character

125 word-guessing entries: 75 complete movie titles and 50 fictional characters. Actor-name entries have been removed as requested. 72 entries are new to the approved 83-word draft; all 53 earlier movie entries are retained.

Every answer contains 5–7 letters. Movie titles are complete, omitting only spaces and punctuation for typing. Character answers use a recognisable given name or nickname, such as BABURAO, CIRCUIT or VIRUS. No film-title fragments or series answers.

Each word has one short Sense hint. The review displays answers for editing; in the game, the board shows the answer type and length, while Sense unlocks the clue. No quiz questions or second hint tier.

Names such as FARHAN, VICKY and ADITYA refer to the fictional roles identified in their hints and references, not the performers. Different roles sharing a name are not added as duplicate words.

Hera Pheri now contributes BABURAO and SHYAM; longer movie titles can still supply recognisable characters. Raid 2 and Sitaare Zameen Par remain excluded.

Approved content reference, imported into the local game on 19 September 2026. These 125 movie/character words replace the previous selection pool in Race and VS. Production deployment is pending. The shortlist includes hits and enduring classics; it is not a uniform box-office verdict claim.

Selected plot, cast and spelling checks are linked beside relevant entries. Coverage is not exhaustive; future factual or spelling corrections should update this reference and the runtime bank together. Only the displayed canonical spelling currently solves each word. Film years identify the release; character years identify the reference movie.
