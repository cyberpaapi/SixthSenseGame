Love daily word puzzles? Sixth Sense challenges you to guess six-letter words in six tries. Follow the clues, tackle Adventure levels, or challenge friends in multiplayer word races. Explore Bollywood puzzles featuring movies and memorable characters.

Work out the hidden word using clues and feedback after every guess. Green Aligned letters are in the right place, orange Echoing letters belong elsewhere, and Quiet letters help narrow the possibilities. Symbols accompany the colors so every result is easier to read.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Take on the Daily puzzle or follow an Adventure through thousands of words. Use Sense for a clue, Peek to reveal an unknown position, and Clear to rule out unused letters. After six guesses, an optional Last Chance offers one final attempt.

CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS
Create a private room and share its code. Race with up to eight players, compete in a two-player VS duel, or solve a shared route in Co-op. Multiplayer requires an internet connection.

BOLLYWOOD WORDS
Play Bollywood Solo at your own pace, or challenge friends in Bollywood Race and Bollywood VS. Guess complete movie titles and memorable characters from a curated collection of 125 answers. Answers use five to seven letters, with short hints available through Sense.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose an animal avatar, switch between light and dark themes, and adjust music and sound effects. Solo progress and coins are saved on your device. Coins are earned in the game and have no cash value.

Solo play is available as a guest. Reserve a unique username to join multiplayer rooms.
