Sixth Sense privacy policy

Developer: Sensei. Updated September 10, 2026.

Contact: aryanchandwani@gmail.com.

Game progress and multiplayer

Solo progress, coins, preferences, selected avatar, and your chosen username are saved on your device. We do not currently offer an account or cloud save for solo progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling can erase it. In private multiplayer rooms, the service processes your chosen username, avatar, gameplay actions, progress, and a device-held room access token to run and reconnect your match. Other room participants can see your username, avatar, progress and permitted game feedback. Do not put sensitive personal information in your username.

Multiplayer uses Vercel hosting and a Neon/Postgres database. Rooms are valid for 24 hours; expired room records may remain in the database until cleanup. Hosting providers process connection information such as IP addresses and technical request logs to operate and secure the service. Contact us with a room code and approximate time if you need help deleting multiplayer data.

Advertising and age choices

The Android app uses Google AdMob for banners on home and below the game lifelines and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (including an advertising identifier when available), IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, app information, and diagnostics for advertising, fraud prevention, and measurement. We request any required consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. Where available, Ad privacy choices in Settings lets you review your choice. Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

The app is intended for ages 13 and above. We store an age group locally, not a date of birth. Under-13 selections disable ads and purchases. Under-18 selections use under-age-of-consent ad settings. We do not intentionally request personalized advertising for minors.

Purchases

Google Play processes the one-time Remove banner ads purchase and handles payment details; Sensei does not receive your card details. The app sends a purchase token over HTTPS to our verification service, which checks and acknowledges the purchase with Google Play. We retain a hash of the token, product identifier, entitlement state and last verification time to support the purchase. These records are separate from multiplayer rooms and retained while needed to support purchases and resolve disputes. Restore purchases checks your Google Play purchases after reinstalling. Optional rewarded ads remain available after banner removal.

Rewards and choices

Watching a reward ad is optional. Extra coins are granted only after the ad SDK reports that the reward was earned. Device-held reward receipts prevent duplicate credits and support recovery after interruption. Coins have no cash value and cannot be purchased, transferred or redeemed for money. You may decline an ad and keep your ordinary solve reward.

To protect the purchase-verification service, we keep a short-lived keyed hash of connection information and request counts to limit excessive requests. These rate-limit records are removed on subsequent requests after one day.

Retention and your rights

You can clear local progress through Android app storage settings or uninstalling. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of service-held data, or with a privacy question. Purchase records may be retained where needed for transaction support or legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may have additional data rights. Service providers may process information outside your country. We use HTTPS in transit and restrict access to server credentials. No security system is absolute.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and update the date above.
