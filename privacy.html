Sixth Sense privacy policy

Developer: Sensei. Updated September 21, 2026.

Contact: aryanchandwani@gmail.com.

Game progress and multiplayer

Solo progress, coins, preferences and selected avatar are saved on your device. We do not currently offer cloud saves for solo progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling can erase it. In private multiplayer rooms, the service processes your chosen username, avatar, gameplay actions, progress, and a device-held room access token to run and reconnect your match. Other room participants can see your username, avatar, progress and permitted game feedback. Do not put sensitive personal information in your username.

To reserve a globally unique username, the service stores a profile identifier, normalized username, display spelling, creation/update dates and a cryptographic hash of your recovery code in its database. Your device stores the recovery code; the service receives it over HTTPS to verify ownership but does not store it in plaintext. Names remain reserved until you rename your profile or request deletion; room expiration does not release them. A recovery code allows access to the username and its linked, unexpired rooms on another browser or device, but does not transfer coins or solo progress. Keep it private. We use short-lived, hashed connection buckets to limit username requests and remove them on later requests after one day.

Multiplayer uses Vercel hosting and a Neon/Postgres database. Rooms are valid for 24 hours; expired room records may remain in the database until cleanup. Hosting providers process connection information such as IP addresses and technical request logs to operate and secure the service. Contact us with a room code and approximate time if you need help deleting multiplayer data.

The updated web game checks a public coin-reset policy periodically and when you return. A new reset sets test wallets to the starting balance while keeping game progress. Wallets with recorded paid packs are excluded from these resets. This check does not upload your wallet or progress; balances remain device-local.

Leaving a room keeps its access key in the same browser so entering the room code again can restore your seat and server-saved progress. Recent keys are retained for up to 32 rooms; keys older than 24 hours since their last save are ignored and removed on a later key save. Clearing site data removes these keys. A username alone cannot restore a seat. Restoring its recovery code can also reconnect a linked profile to an unexpired room; this replaces that room's old access key.

During an active multiplayer match, the game reports when its tab or app goes into the background and when you return. Room participants receive an away alert and a red avatar marker while you are away. Departure events and current screen status are kept with the temporary room data. We do not inspect other apps or websites; calls and screen locks may also cause an away alert. The game's own ad flows are excluded where supported. These signals are not proof of cheating.

Advertising and age choices

The Android app uses Google AdMob for banners on home and below the game lifelines and optional rewarded ads, plus automatic full-screen ads after a multiplayer match and after every three newly completed Adventure levels. Remove Ads disables banners and automatic ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (including an advertising identifier when available), IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, app information, and diagnostics for advertising, fraud prevention, and measurement. We request any required consent using Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. Where available, Ad privacy choices in Settings lets you review your choice. Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

The app is intended for ages 13 and above. We store an age group locally, not a date of birth. Under-13 selections disable ads and purchases. Under-18 selections use under-age-of-consent ad settings. We do not intentionally request personalized advertising for minors.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases and handles payment details; Sensei does not receive your card details. Remove Ads and the Complete Pack are one-time permanent purchases. Coin packs and Lifeline Kits can be purchased repeatedly. The app displays prices returned by Google Play for your region. Optional rewarded ads remain available after ad removal.

The app sends the product identifier, purchase token and a random installation identifier over HTTPS to our verification service. This identifier is also passed to Google Play as an obfuscated account identifier to bind supply delivery to the buying installation. The service verifies payment, acknowledges permanent products and consumes repeatable products after delivery. It retains hashed purchase tokens, product identifiers, installation identifiers and delivery timestamps to prevent duplicate supply grants and support purchases. Raw purchase tokens are not stored in our database. The device retains pending tokens privately until delivery is completed.

Coin and lifeline supplies are stored on this installation. They do not transfer or restore after clearing app data or reinstalling; permanent ad removal restores through Google Play. The Complete Pack's supplies are granted once on the original installation, while its ad-removal benefit can restore on another device with the same Play account. Keep your Play receipt and contact support for unresolved delivery. Purchase records are separate from multiplayer rooms and retained as needed for support, fraud prevention, disputes and legal obligations.

Rewards and choices

Watching a reward ad is optional. Extra coins are granted only after the ad SDK reports that the reward was earned. Device-held reward receipts prevent duplicate credits and support recovery after interruption. Coins may be earned or purchased, have no cash value, and cannot be transferred or redeemed for money. You may decline an ad and keep your ordinary solve reward.

To protect the purchase-verification service, we keep a short-lived keyed hash of connection information and request counts to limit excessive requests. These rate-limit records are removed on subsequent requests after one day.

Retention and your rights

You can clear local progress through Android app storage settings or uninstalling. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of service-held data, or with a privacy question. Purchase records may be retained where needed for transaction support or legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may have additional data rights. Service providers may process information outside your country. We use HTTPS in transit and restrict access to server credentials. No security system is absolute.
