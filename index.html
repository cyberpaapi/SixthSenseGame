How to play

Uncover one common six-letter word in seven tries. Every guess must be a valid six-letter word.

P●LA◆NE×T
  • Aligned ● means the letter belongs exactly there.
  • Echoing ◆ means the letter is in the answer, somewhere else.
  • Quiet × means the letter does not appear in the answer.
Five ways to play: Daily gives everyone the same word. Practice is unlimited. Sprint gives you 90 seconds. Insight begins with a clue and revealed position. Streak chains consecutive wins.
