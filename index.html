How to play

Uncover one common six-letter word in seven tries. Every guess must be a valid six-letter word.

P●LA◆NE×T
  • Aligned ● means the letter belongs exactly there.
  • Echoing ◆ means the letter is in the answer, somewhere else.
  • Quiet × means the letter does not appear in the answer.
Seven ways to play: Daily gives everyone the same word. Practice is unlimited. Time Tackle gives you ten minutes. Insight begins with a clue and revealed position. Streak chains consecutive wins. Race puts 2–8 players on a shared 3, 5, or 10-word track. VS is a 3, 5, 9, or Endless round duel: first correct guess wins the point while both players see live color patterns.
